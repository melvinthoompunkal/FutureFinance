# Phase 1 feasibility

Reviewed November 21, 2026. The local prototype and its scenario checks are ready. This phase stays open until the intended data account or an approved replacement export supplies the outstanding inputs.

## Starting scope

Use SPY as the benchmark, QQQ as the comparison, and the [11 sector ETFs](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/capabilities/equities/sector-investing/sector-and-industry-etfs) for relative performance. Use the [20-symbol watchlist](../watchlist.txt) for the issuer pilot. Use dated IVV equity holdings as the initial membership candidate and label it **IVV holdings breadth**. Use licensed S&P membership if available, with one vote per listed share class in the chosen dated universe. The planned host is the current Windows workstation with a $0 data budget. The regular-session cutoff is 4:30 p.m. New York time. The eventual collector also needs the exchange holiday and early-close calendar.

## Measure definitions

All price calculations end at the selected completed session. Use the observed, published and collected times as distinct fields. When a point-in-time vintage is unknown, label the data reconstructed. Express differences of returns or margins as percentage points where appropriate.

| Measure | Unit and window | Missing or eligibility rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quoted close and change | USD quoted close. One-session percentage change on split-adjusted closes. | Require both adjacent split-adjusted closes for the change. |
| Market and stock trend | Percent from the 20/50/200-session simple moving averages on split-adjusted closes. Percentage change in the 200-session average over 20 sessions. | Require a complete moving-average window. Both 50 and 200 gaps positive give uptrend, both negative downtrend, other valid combinations mixed. |
| Market and sector breadth | Count member share classes with close strictly above each 50/200-session average. Divide by eligible members and show five-session change in percentage points. | Require a verified dated universe and complete price history per member per average. Below 90% eligibility show coverage in place of a breadth label. At or above 90%, at least 60% is broad and at most 40% is narrow. |
| Relative and absolute return | Percent change over 5/20/60 sessions in the adjusted price ratio and percent change in dividend-adjusted close. Chart rebases series to 100 at the first common date. | Require both endpoints on common sessions. For stock/sector, withhold when no dated sector map exists. Sector outperformance counts members with positive 20-session change in member/SPY, with eligible and expected counts. |
| Volatility and credit | VIX and VIX3M in index points, common-date VIX/VIX3M ratio, OAS in bp and its change over 20 observations. VIX historical context uses percent of trailing-year observations below its current level. Credit proxy is the 20-session adjusted HYG/IEF change in percent. | Show observation dates and use values published by the cutoff. Require both volatility legs, available OAS endpoints, or both proxy ETF series as applicable. |
| Stock risk and volume | Percent distance to 252-session split-adjusted intraday high. ATR is the mean of 14 split-adjusted true ranges divided by split-adjusted close. Realized volatility is sample standard deviation of 20/60 daily log returns × √252. Drawdown is percent from the running split-adjusted high over the chart window. Relative volume is same-feed comparable shares divided by the preceding 20-session mean. | Require sufficient complete history, consistent adjusted OHLC and volume bases, and a positive volume denominator. These inputs still need an entitled OHLCV sample or export. |
| Business health | Same fiscal-quarter revenue change YoY (%), operating income/revenue (%) and its prior-year difference (pp), four standalone quarters of CFO less capex (USD). Leverage uses gross interest-bearing debt / trailing EBITDA, and coverage uses trailing EBIT / positive interest expense. | Require accession-linked values published by the cutoff and comparable fiscal periods. Derive standalone quarters from compatible reporting periods. Mark inapplicable ratios for financial issuers or nonpositive denominators. |
| Valuation and earnings | Quoted close / positive trailing diluted EPS, FCF / market capitalization (%), and next earnings date with confirmation status. Peer comparisons are the same measure at the same cutoff with median and count. | Mark negative EPS loss-making. Zero or missing EPS, unmatched shares, incomparable peer accounting, and unknown earnings dates have explicit unavailable states. |

## Walkthrough

- **Market, Aug 8, 2025.** Synthetic SPY rose 0.84% and ended in an uptrend. There were 38 of 100 eligible members above their 50-session average, down 7 pp in five sessions. The chart shows benchmark performance above the breadth chart, with both counts and trend in the evidence column.
- **Sector.** Select XLK from the sector ranking. Inspect relative and absolute returns and open AAPL from the sector members. The chart window and session stay selected.
- **Stock.** Synthetic AAPL rose 1.30% while operating margin declined from 30.0% to 28.0% year over year. Synthetic RIVN displays loss-making trailing earnings after the simulated filing cutoff. CRCL retains the SPY comparison and reports fewer than 200 available closes.
